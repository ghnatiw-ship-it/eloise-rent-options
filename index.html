New Rent Strategy

How the landlord sees it. Robert judges a deal by the total rent he collects over the lease, not by any single year. That total is his sticking point: he will move on timing, but not on giving up much of the overall return.

What we need. Our priority is buying time: lower rent in the next two years, so Eloise can keep growing without the partners putting more money in. The new offers do that by moving rent later in the lease. His total stays close to the lease, and the relief comes up front where we need it.

The trade-off. Rent moved later makes the long-term plan aggressive: sales have to keep climbing every year to carry it. We have done this before. Scotland Yard grew from 2006 onward, and Bar Cathedral has grown since 2022.
